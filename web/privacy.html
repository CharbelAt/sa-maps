SA Maps Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

SA Maps is a hobby/lab navigation app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not sell or share personal data. This page lists exactly what leaves your device and why.

Location

  • Your location is used on your device to show where you are and to follow your route, including while navigating with the screen off (a notification is shown while this happens).
  • When you ask for a route, your current position and your destination are sent to the routing service (routing.openstreetmap.de, run by FOSSGIS; fallback: OSRM demo server) to calculate the route.
  • To keep search results local, your approximate position is sent along with search text to Photon (by Komoot), and once to look up your country.
  • Location is not stored on any SA Maps server.

Search and place details

  • What you type in the search box is sent to Photon to find places.
  • When you open a place, its name and coordinates are sent to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) to fetch opening hours, phone and website.

Map

Map tiles and fonts are downloaded from OpenFreeMap. Like any website, these services see your IP address when your device requests data from them. Their own privacy policies apply.

Voices

The text of each spoken direction (for example "In 300 meters, turn left onto Hamra Street") is sent to the SA Maps voice server to turn it into audio. The server keeps generated audio in a cache and keeps short technical logs (time, IP address, request) for operation and abuse prevention. It does not receive your coordinates.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to the SA Maps server: the error details, app version, phone model, Android version and the IP address the report came from. Reports are used only to fix bugs.

Music controls (optional)

If you grant notification access, SA Maps reads what your music app is playing (title, artist, cover art) to show playback controls. This information stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. SA Maps does not read the contents of your notifications.

Data stored on your device

Settings, saved places (home, work, favorites, recent destinations) and optional sound clips you add are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Children

SA Maps is not directed at children.

Contact
